Atlas Source Registry

A searchable, rights-aware GTA research directory. Our goal is to build a locally managed data collection—not depend on copying and repeatedly fetching other fan sites.

Central source credits: Read Credits, Maps & Notices for current creators, licenses, original links, image and pre-release restrictions. This technical registry is a research index, not separate reuse permission.
Usage rule: Being a fan site, allowing public browsing, or not explicitly forbidding copying does not grant redistribution rights. Check the exact license on each dataset, artwork asset, tile set, and repository. Credit alone does not replace permission. Noncommercial/share-alike licenses may be unsuitable for commercial Elario use.

Data ingestion standard

Store each approved record with a stable ID, GTA title, mode (Story/Online/VI), category, label, coordinates (if verified), verification status, source URL, attribution/license, and last-checked date. Validate coordinate bounds, duplicates, and provenance before presenting pins. Keep pre-release GTA VI reconstructions clearly marked as provisional.

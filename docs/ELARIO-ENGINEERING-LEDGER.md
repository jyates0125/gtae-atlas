# Elario Studios — engineering and investment ledger

Version 1.0 · November 26, 2026

Record dated, attributable work without inflating hours or claiming unverified authorship. Use a private ledger for personal timesheets, contracts, expenses, confidential research and intellectual-property evidence. This public-safe template contains no sensitive information.

## Record template
| Date / timezone | Project | Task / decision | Human contribution (specific) | AI assistance | Evidence (commit, artifact, tests) | Human active minutes | AI active minutes if measurable | CI machine minutes | Direct expenses | Verification / next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD / offset | Project | Task | Actual authored code, edits, decisions or review | Generated suggestions, code, research | Exact reference | Actual or unknown | Actual or unknown | Runner-reported or unknown | Actual or unknown | Outcome and limits |

Do not add concurrent human, AI or CI time as if they were equivalent human hours. Separate active time, wall-clock intervals, machine consumption and estimated labor value. Preserve unknown values rather than inventing timesheets. Keep evidence of design choices, human-authored code and revisions, testing, approvals, source license and migration/backup checks.

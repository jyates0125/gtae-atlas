# Elario Studios — intellectual property and community collaboration

Version 1.0 · November 26, 2026 · Owner-directed operating policy; not legal counsel review or a representation of completed incorporation, registration or IP assignment.

## Governing principles
Reserve original protectable rights lawfully held by the applicable owner. Maintain an ownership and provenance register, including human authorship, AI-assisted work, contributor agreements and assignments where applicable. Do not claim ownership of third-party assets, facts, ideas, or unprotectable material.

Product licenses are scoped, explicit and separately approved. Do not grant a blanket MIT or Creative Commons license to Elario's portfolio. Respect previously granted licenses, third-party notices, statutory rights and platform terms. Keep confidential technology in private repositories with appropriate access controls; public source is not a trade secret.

GTAE is a noncommercial independent fan companion with existing limited guide-excerpt reuse permission, not a blanket open-source project. Its [rights notice](../LICENSE.md), [reuse policy](../REUSE-POLICY.md) and [third-party notices](../NOTICE.md) implement this policy for GTAE only. Separate private Forge work and future commercial software may use independently cleared technology and assets; do not assume Rockstar fan permissions cover commercial products. Other projects require their own rights review and licensing decisions.

## Community and future business
Welcome rights-cleared contributions, corrections and feedback. Identify contributor rights and obtain appropriate written grants or assignments before merging material where the existing permission is insufficient. Do not assume a submission assigns ownership or authorizes relicensing; do not change existing contributor terms retroactively. Keep source-specific open licenses intact. Consider releasing selected standalone utilities or educational materials under compatible standard licenses only after owner approval and dependency/rights review.

Maintain clear permission and takedown processes, verified private contacts, and versioned public notices. Reserve the right to address misuse where legally justified without overriding lawful exceptions or already granted licenses.

Before commercialization, investment or major distribution, document legal entity and chain of title, perform trademark clearance and consider registration of eligible works, secure required third-party permissions, and obtain qualified legal review. No monetization, authentication or private technology transfer is authorized by this policy.

# Elario Studios — founder, technology and education principles

Version 1.0 · November 26, 2026 · Owner-approved operating direction; legal implementation and actual ownership remain subject to evidence and qualified review.

**Mission:** Build useful technology, advance AI-assisted learning, develop sustainable independent businesses, and create opportunities for future generations. Technology · AI · Education · Innovation. Many Crews. One Vision.

## Founder control and lawful ownership
- Document the founder's actual original contributions and existing ownership before considering assignment or licensing to an entity. No automatic transfer of personal intellectual property, equity or control; neither this charter nor an AI assistant's participation creates a partnership, assignment or registered right.
- Keep original protectable code, designs, documentation, arrangements and other rights reserved unless a specific release is authorized. Maintain existing third-party licenses, GitHub terms and lawful exceptions. Do not imply that facts, ideas, purely AI-generated expression or another rights holder's work are exclusively owned.
- Before investment, entity formation, equity agreements, patent disclosures, commercial licensing or material transfers, obtain qualified legal/tax review and documented authorization. Record actual ownership and assignments separately in restricted records.
- Keep confidential algorithms, engines, commercial research, credentials and trade-secret material in access-controlled repositories. Maintain portable formats, independent verified backups, dependency inventories and migration paths. Evaluate inventions before public disclosure.

## Human-directed, AI-assisted engineering and education
- Teach practical programming, software architecture, testing, troubleshooting, research, accessibility and security through explainable examples and rights-cleared learning material. The founder directs and participates in development; record specific human-authored code, designs, decisions and modifications separately from assistant suggestions or generated output.
- Keep dated engineering records of actual contributions, test results, source provenance, approvals and limitations. Never attribute machine-generated output to a human without evidence or equate elapsed AI/CI time with human labor.
- Encourage learners to understand and build independently rather than copy proprietary systems. Publish selected standalone educational examples or utilities only with owner approval and a compatible, explicitly scoped license. No blanket MIT or Creative Commons license for Elario or GTAE.
- Preserve GTAE's existing limited noncommercial guide-excerpt permission, fan independence, guest privacy and source-specific credits. Separate commercial products from third-party game IP. No authentication, monetization or new data collection is authorized by this document.

## Brand and creative records
- Approved direction: angular gold E for corporate Elario Studios identity; winged E for premium enterprise/creative use; crowned E for Team Jura/GTAE gaming identity. Preserve existing approved artwork and do not replace the live brand without a separately reviewed asset update.
- Use © notices only for rights lawfully held, with appropriate authorship/AI limitations; ™ may indicate an unregistered mark claim, not registration or clearance. Do not use ® without applicable registration. Perform clearance before formal adoption/registration.
- Record source, date, production method, human contributions, license basis, asset hash and intended use; keep editable masters and original files independently backed up. See [creative register](ELARIO-CREATIVE-REGISTER.md).
- Maintain the [engineering and investment ledger](ELARIO-ENGINEERING-LEDGER.md). Keep personal timesheets, sensitive financial data, ownership evidence and confidential technology out of public repositories.

This supplements the [rights operating policy](ELARIO-RIGHTS-POLICY.md), not a license expansion or legal opinion. Rights and control are grounded in actual law, authorship, contracts and documented ownership.

05 / Notice & evidence standard

How we label what visitors see

Official / Rockstar-confirmed

Claims with directly linked Rockstar announcements or rights-holder documentation. A factual announcement does not itself license associated game images.

Community R&D

Independently traced maps, source-backed data, community coordinates or academic-style measurements with credited creators and visible limitations.

Unverified / Not exact representation

Inferred POIs, approximate coordinates, possible collectible/spawn points or pre-release Leonida reconstruction. Some community source research includes leak-derived information. Atlas does not publish the actual leaked game footage.

Elario original / Illustrative

Original crew artwork, map covers, user-generated pins, custom concept vehicles and cinematic scenes. These should never be mistaken for official game screenshots or released game assets.

Fan-community policy: Rockstar's published policy states that Take-Two generally does not object to noncommercial fan use that avoids intentional story spoilers. It also reserves removal rights, excludes pre-release leaked footage, and is framed for occasional individual fan content; it does not automatically cover film distribution or Elario commercial promotion. Read the official policy ↗
Rights boundary. Attribution is necessary where required, but does not create permission. MIT/Apache/Creative Commons terms cover their licensors' own contributions—not the original Rockstar maps, characters, radar art or embedded media. If a source expressly forbids reuse, do not import it; for uncertain assets, use source links, an original Elario alternative, or seek authorization before public distribution.

Storage and external links: User progress, markers and imported classic images stay in browser-managed storage where supported. Local JSON exports of classic notes do not automatically include a separately stored high-resolution raster. External image providers and linked pages have independent privacy policies; no Rockstar account sign-in is provided.

Future film / monetization: Rockstar's fan-content guidance is conditional and does not universally cover studio promotion, independently distributed films or Elario's future family projects. Those remain separate and require their own asset and rights review.

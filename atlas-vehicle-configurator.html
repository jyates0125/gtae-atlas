What you're looking at

The Forge uses our own sculpted, class-specific WebGL geometry. It is an original concept, not an exact production game mesh. Selecting a named GTA vehicle changes the reference image and the design workspace, not the 3D mesh into a Rockstar or GTADB game model. The 3D idea is a paint/rotation concept tool, and available GTA V images are credited reference material.

Current customization coverage

Our original viewer supports 3D rotation, zoom, original class-specific concepts, camera presets, surface finishes, accents, lighting, road stance and aero or deck styling. Per-model wheel meshes, liveries, spoilers, true Rockstar assets and precise modification catalogs are not available in this original concept tool. Those need separately licensed model files and verified metadata before they can be represented faithfully.

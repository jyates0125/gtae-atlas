Source quality and attribution

Collection totals: 100 tags (Los Santos), 50 snapshots (San Fierro), 50 horseshoes (Las Venturas), 50 oysters (statewide), 70 optional unique stunt jumps. 150 mapped game-world positions derive from Flentric SA.MapCollectibles (MIT licensed source), converted by x=gameX+3000, y=3000-gameY for the Toliak vector artwork (Apache-2.0). Alignment remains approximate. Original game-world materials are © Rockstar Games / Take-Two. No locations from a source-uncertain third-party map were copied. The first four sets count toward 100% completion. GTA collectible reference · Sourced San Andreas map. External resources are credited rather than scraped.

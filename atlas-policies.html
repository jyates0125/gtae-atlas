Clear policies. Your choices.

These notices explain what Atlas does today and what remains planned. You may explore without creating an account. This is a public development preview; secure account sign-in and cloud synchronization have not been activated.

01 / PRIVACY

We do not sell your game progress or account information.

Elario Studios does not sell visitor account registration information or saved game progress. Atlas currently offers guest play and manual local backups; this account page does not ask for an email address or password. If optional accounts launch, we plan to use only information necessary to authenticate a user, protect the service, and synchronize the saves they choose.

Website hosting, security and infrastructure providers may process standard connection data such as IP addresses, device/browser information and access logs to operate and protect the site. External linked websites and embedded or third-party resources may have their own practices. We will inventory and identify actual providers, data categories and retention schedules before enabling account collection, analytics or advertising.

A user-facing private contact method and verified rights request process must be published before secure accounts go live. Do not submit personal information in public repository issues.

02 / GUEST PROGRESS

Guest mode is free. Your browser holds your progress.

Visitors can use maps, collectibles and My Garage without an Atlas account. Progress for these tools is saved in browser-managed storage on that device and browser. Clearing site data, using a different browser, losing or replacing a phone, resetting a device or browser storage eviction can permanently remove unsynced progress. Elario Studios cannot restore a deleted local guest save from its servers.

Back up your Atlas progress using the existing manual export and restore controls. Exported files are your responsibility to safeguard. They are not automatically synchronized, and importing a backup may overwrite matching local progress after confirmation.

When secure sign-in is independently tested and enabled, optional account-based syncing can make progress available on other devices. Guest saves will not be automatically uploaded or silently merged; transfer will require an informed choice. At present, sign-in and automatic cloud syncing are not available.

03 / FUTURE ACCOUNT DELETION

Our proposed seven-day account recovery window

When accounts launch, we intend to provide a verified request to delete an account, disable access while the request is pending, allow a seven-day cancellation window to prevent accidental loss, and then delete the account and associated cloud saves through a tested server-side process. Seven days is an Elario Studios proposed recovery choice, not a legal rule requiring us to retain accounts for seven days.

Final deletion timing must account for real identity, storage, security logs, backups and any applicable legal obligations. Limited records may sometimes be retained for security, lawful compliance or deletion-request records; we will state the actual categories, purposes and timeframes before account registration is enabled. We will not promise complete removal of every backup in seven days until that can be verified.

Deleting a future cloud account does not erase separate manual JSON backups or local device copies. The deletion button and seven-day automated workflow do not yet exist on the public site; no one is currently required to register with Team Jura Atlas.

07 / LOCAL STORAGE & EXTERNAL LINKS

Saving progress is optional.

Browser storage supports guest collections, Garage notes, local marker layouts and manual backups. Some features may store personal map images locally. Before launching optional analytics, personalized advertising or new nonessential tracking, we will review consent requirements and update this disclosure.
